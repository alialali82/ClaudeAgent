# Little Bear and the Unfinished Dark — Crayon Picture Book

**Logline.** A small bear is afraid of the dark, which waits in the cave as a big scribble with no face; on the night he has to walk through it, the page draws itself one step ahead of him, and the dark turns out to be full of colour.

> **Revision note.** My first treatment spent the style's *wax resist*: a bear draws invisible white-crayon stars all day and a watercolour wash reveals them. The library demo (`styles/crayon-book/DEMO.md`) is exactly that story, on one page, ending with the page seen on a table, so I rebuilt this film. What follows is the film as delivered.

## Three candidate structures
1. **One page, one reveal** (stars under a wash) — the demo's structure. Rejected.
2. **A flip through pages**, one stage per page joined by page turns: dusk → night → clearing → the finished page on the fridge. **Chosen.**
3. **A colouring-book page** — outlines first, courage colours one area at a time. Rejected: no moment where the world *acts*.

**Arc.** Setup — dusk page; a bear who loves the day and fears the cave (0–18 s). Turn — page turn to the night page; the world draws itself ahead of him, light by light, as he walks and the boil calms (19–37 s). Payoff — page turn to the clearing; the dark bursts into colour; he is happy (38–50 s). Ending — the page is taped to a fridge with other drawings; "And Little Bear slept very well." (50–60 s).

## Benchmark
Learn: the hand-textured, breathing frames of a wordless winter picture-book film. Don't take: characters, melody or imagery.

## Crayon box (10) — no watercolour
Indigo line, night indigo, violet, brown, tan, orange, yellow, pink, grass green, dark green, russet. (The film has no wash.)

## Cue map and sound design
6/8 at dotted-quarter = 84 (0.714 s/beat). Ukulele and toy piano by day; low clarinet and a music box winding down in the fear; a single bass note as a heartbeat at the page turn; one celesta note per light as it is drawn; the theme (recorder + ukulele) is withheld until 40 s, then played once in full; a music box slows to a stop on the fridge. Foley: crayon stick-slip per stroke, colouring swishes, felt steps, page turns (lift, whoosh, flap), tape. Crickets thin out in the fear and return with the lights.

## Subtitle and title design
Printed words of the book: Patrick Hand ~54 px, indigo wax, each glyph tilted, boiling on twos, written left to right in 0.35 s on a ragged knock-out patch of paper. Title hand-lettered in Gaegu, written left to right.

## Native moves used
The world draws itself ahead of the hero · colouring is an action (the burst) · boil, calmed as the film quiets · child logic (a scribble with no face, noodle arms) · page turn · it is a book (the fridge).
