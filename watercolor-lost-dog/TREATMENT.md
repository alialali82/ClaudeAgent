# The Long Way Home — Watercolor Brush

**Logline.** A lost dog walks home through a painted world that only exists a few strokes ahead of him; the colder and farther he goes the less the painter can hold, until the page is washed blank — and one warm smell paints the way back.

## Three candidate structures
1. **A single lateral journey** on one long sheet (handscroll): the dog walks left→right; the world paints in just ahead of him; the palette itself carries the feeling (cold blue-grey → warm ochre).
2. **A field journal**: one locked page per landmark (market, bridge, field), each with hand notes, turned like pages. Tidy but static; a dog needs to *travel*.
3. **Painted backwards**: open on the finished house, watch it unpaint to blank paper, then the dog paints it back. Clever, but it gives away the ending in the hook.

**Chosen: 1**, with the mist-erase from structure 3 at the low point. The first idea for "lost dog" is rain and a street lamp; I keep the rain but make *the page itself* the thing that gets lost (mist erases it), and the way home is a smell: warm ochre arrives as colour before the house does.

**Arc.** Setup — a blot of wet brown on blank paper becomes a small dog with a vermilion tag (0–7 s). Turn — the cold city and river exhaust him; mist wipes the world back to paper; silence (33–41 s). Ending — a faint warm wash, then a lane, then a door; the tag is the one red thing on the page, and it is the last thing we hear (41–60 s).

## Benchmark
Learn: naturalist field-journal plates (specimen + hand note, generous paper margins); long horizontal handscrolls (one continuous painted world, camera never cuts); the "transect" gradient read across space. Don't take: any specific artwork, layout or character.

## Beat sheet / cue map
72 bpm (0.833 s/beat), 4/4. Sections: A blank (0–7) · B street (7–20) · C bridge (20–32) · D mist (32–41, music out, 2 silences) · E lane (41–52) · F door (52–60).
Narration (felt piano, kalimba, soft strings, brush rustle): lines at 2.0, 9.5, 17.0, 24.0, (silence 32–41), 41.5, 46.5, 53.5.
